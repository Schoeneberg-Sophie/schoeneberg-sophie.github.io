This project is an independent academic/portfolio research effort analyzing

manufacturer referenced here.

**Data source & accuracy:** Camera location data is sourced from 
[OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) via the community-run 
[DeFlock](https://deflock.me) project, which relies on volunteer contributions. 
This data may be incomplete, outdated, or inaccurate, and should not be relied 
upon as an authoritative or exhaustive record of ALPR camera deployments. 
Absence of a camera from this dataset does not mean no camera is present at 
that location, and its presence does not guarantee the camera is active, 
correctly categorized, or still installed.

**Purpose:** This project exists to visualize and study the geographic 
footprint of automated license plate recognition (ALPR) infrastructure in 
public spaces, as a technical/data-visualization exercise. It makes no claims 
about the legality, effectiveness, policies, or conduct of any company, 
government agency, or law enforcement body that operates or contracts these 
systems. Any such entities named here are named solely for descriptive/ 
identification purposes.

**No legal or safety advice:** This repository is provided for educational 
and informational purposes only. It is not intended to facilitate evasion of 
law enforcement, and nothing here should be construed as legal advice.
