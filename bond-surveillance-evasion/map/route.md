The route James Bond takes in the 1985 movie A View to a Kill:

1. Fisherman's Wharf
   Bond meets CIA Agent Chuck LEE at Tarantino's Restaurant - 206 Jefferson Street. They walk to the docks.
2. San Francisco City Hall
   1 dr Carlton B Goodlet Place - Bond and Stacey Sutton research Zorin, the building is set on fire.
3. Fire Truck Chase
   Starts at City Hall. Famous hook-and-ladder sequence was filmed at the intersection of Market and California.
4. Golden Gate Bridge
   End of route. Bond grabs the mooring line of Zorins airship.

roughly 4-5 miles through downtown San Francisco
